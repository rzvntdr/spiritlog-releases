# SpiritLog — Privacy Policy

_Effective: September 29, 2026_

SpiritLog is a meditation timer that keeps track of your practice. It is built
by one independent developer, has no server of its own, and does not collect
your data. This page explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how to
remove it.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you create in SpiritLog is stored on your phone:

- your meditation sessions (date, length, preset used),
- your presets, sounds and reminder times,
- your streak, your plant and your achievements,
- your settings.

The app also keeps diagnostic log files on your phone for 30 days. They
describe what the app did (for example "session started", "backup finished")
and never leave the phone.

Uninstalling SpiritLog deletes all of this.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to turn on backup, you sign in with your Google account. SpiritLog
then:

- sees your name and email address, only to show which account is connected;
- gets access to a **hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only SpiritLog
  can see** (Google's "app data" folder). It cannot see, open or change any of
  your other Drive files;
- saves a copy of your SpiritLog data there when you tap "Back up now", or
  after each session if you turn on automatic backup. It keeps one copy per day
  for the last 14 days, so you can restore them on a new phone.

Your backups go straight from your phone to your Google Drive. The developer
never receives them and cannot read them. Google handles them under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

To remove backups: sign out in SpiritLog, then in Google Drive open
**Settings → Manage apps → SpiritLog → Options → Delete hidden app data**. You
can also revoke SpiritLog's access at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Android may also include SpiritLog in your phone's own backup to your Google
account, depending on your phone's backup settings. That backup is run by
Android, not by SpiritLog.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to show reminders and the notification of a running
  session. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.
- **Do Not Disturb access** (optional): to silence your phone during a session
  if you ask for it. SpiritLog does not read your notifications.
- **Foreground service, wake lock**: so the timer and its sounds keep running
  with the screen off.
- **Internet**: only for Google sign-in and Drive backup.
- **Run at startup**: to restore your reminders and the home-screen widget
  after the phone restarts.

## What SpiritLog does not do

- No ads.
- No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- No account with the developer: the only sign-in is Google, and only for
  backup.

## Children

SpiritLog is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
anything from them. It doesn't collect anything from anyone.
